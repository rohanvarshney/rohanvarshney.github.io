FAQ

How far in advance should I book?
Peak wedding and holiday dates go fast — 3–6 months ahead is ideal. For club nights and private events, a few weeks is usually fine. Send an inquiry either way; I’ll tell you right away if the date’s open.
Do you take requests and do-not-play lists?
Absolutely. For weddings and private events we’ll do a planning call to cover must-plays, do-not-plays, and key moments (entrances, first dance, performances). Live requests are welcome when they fit the vibe.
Do you provide sound and lighting?
I’m happy to plug my Serato rig straight into the venue’s system. If your space doesn’t have built-in audio, share your room size and guest count in your inquiry and I can coordinate speakers, subs, uplighting, and wireless mics to fit the event.
Can you play choreographed performances and custom edits?
Yes — backed by years of production and practice. Send me your performance tracks ahead of time, or I can cut custom edits and mashups for entrances and special moments. See Production.
Do you travel?
I’m based in Brooklyn, NYC and primarily play across the five boroughs and the Tri-State area. For events outside NYC, reach out with the location and we can work out travel details.
What are your rates?
Every event is different (length, gear, travel), so I quote per event. Share a few details in the form below and you’ll get a clear quote, usually within 48 hours.
