FAQ

How far in advance should I book?
Weddings and Navratri dates go fast — 3–6 months ahead is ideal. For club nights and private events, a few weeks is usually fine. Send an inquiry either way; I’ll tell you right away if the date’s open.
Do you take requests and do-not-play lists?
Absolutely. For weddings and private events we’ll do a planning call to cover must-plays, do-not-plays, and key moments (entrances, first dance, performances). Live requests are welcome when they fit the vibe.
Do you provide sound and lighting?
I’m happy to plug into the venue’s system. If you need a full setup — speakers, subs, uplighting, wireless mics — I can provide or coordinate one. [Update with your packages.]
Can you play choreographed performances and custom edits?
Yes — that’s my background. Send me your performance tracks ahead of time, or I can cut custom edits and mashups for entrances and dances. See Production.
Do you travel?
Yes. I’m based in [City, ST] and regularly play across [region]. Out-of-town events are welcome — travel is quoted separately.
What are your rates?
Every event is different (length, gear, travel), so I quote per event. Share a few details in the form below and you’ll get a clear quote, usually within 48 hours.
