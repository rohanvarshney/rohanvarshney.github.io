FAQ

How much does a project cost?
It depends on scope, since a DJ edit is very different from a full original. Share the details below, and I’ll send a flat quote up front. Artist collabs can also be structured as splits instead of a fee.
What’s the typical turnaround?
Edits and mashups: about 1 week. Performance mixes and remixes: 2–4 weeks. Originals vary. Rush timelines are possible; just mention your deadline.
Do I get stems and project files?
You’ll always get final masters. Stems are available on request; project files depend on the agreement.
How do credits and splits work?
For releases, we agree on credits, splits, and ownership in writing before anything goes out. For commissioned work (intros and performance mixes), you’re free to use it as agreed, with credit to ROVA.
Can we work remotely?
Yes, most projects are fully remote via shared folders, voice notes, and timestamped feedback. In-person sessions in NYC are welcome too.

Got an idea? Let’s make it hit.

Artists, DJs, performers, and brands: tell me what you’re working on. Rough voice memos and reference links are more than welcome.
